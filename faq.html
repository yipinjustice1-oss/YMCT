常见问题解答Frequently Asked Questions

关于古筝课程、体验课、会员与练习场地，您想了解的都在这里。 Everything you might want to know about our guzheng lessons, trial classes, membership, and practice room access.

我完全没有基础，可以报名体验课吗？ I have zero experience — can I still book a trial class?

当然可以。体验课专为零基础学员设计，无论是孩子还是成人都能轻松上手。 Absolutely. Trial classes are designed for complete beginners, whether you're a child or an adult.

上课前需要自己先买古筝吗？ Do I need to buy a guzheng before I start lessons?

不需要。体验课与初期课程可先使用馆内古筝，确定孩子有兴趣后再考虑购买。如需选购，可参考我们的古筝商城与选购指南。 No — trial and early lessons can use an in-studio instrument. Once you've confirmed genuine interest, browse our guzheng shop and buying guide to choose one.

体验课多少钱，上多久？ How much does a trial class cost, and how long is it?

具体价格与时长请见体验课页面。 See our trial class page for current pricing and length.

你们教什么年龄层？ What age groups do you teach?

孩子与成人都欢迎，课程节奏会根据学员年龄与程度调整。详见儿童古筝课程与成人古筝课程。 Both children and adults are welcome — lessons are paced to the student's age and level. See our kids guzheng and adult guzheng pages.

孩子担心无法坚持，是否可以直接按月支付？ Worried the child might not persist — can we pay monthly?

可以。除年度会员预付特惠外，我们提供弹性月付方案，同时会员协议内明确标注合理的退费与转让规则。 Yes. Besides the annual prepaid discount, we offer flexible monthly plans, with reasonable refund and transfer rules clearly outlined in the membership agreement.

陪练老师的具体服务模式是怎样的？ How does the practice-room teacher support work?

课余时间到馆练琴时，驻场陪练老师将全程提供手型纠正、节奏指导与视唱练耳辅助，确保每一次练习都精准有效。 When you drop by to practice, an on-site teacher provides continuous posture correction, rhythm guidance, and ear-training support, so every practice session is precise and effective.

有登台演出的机会吗？ Are there real performance opportunities?

有。我们定期举办国际大师班与正式音乐会演出机会，帮助学员通过舞台实战积累自信与荣誉感。 Yes — we regularly hold international masterclasses and formal concert performances, helping students build real confidence and pride through actual stage experience.
