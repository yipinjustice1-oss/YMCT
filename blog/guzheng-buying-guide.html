新手如何选购第一台古筝？ How to Choose Your First Guzheng

许多家长在为孩子选购第一台古筝时，往往只比较价格，却忽略了真正影响音色与手感的关键因素。这篇指南将帮助您理解古筝价格背后的差异，做出更合适的选择。 Many parents shopping for their child's first guzheng compare price alone, missing the factors that actually determine tone and playability. This guide breaks down what drives guzheng pricing so you can make a better-informed choice.

1. 木料等级决定音色基础1. Wood Grade Shapes the Tone

古筝的面板通常使用泡桐木（桐木），不同产地与生长年份的桐木密度不同，直接影响共鸣与音色饱满度。入门琴多使用普通桐木拼板，而专业演奏级古筝则采用整块优质桐木手工雕刻而成，音色更通透持久。 A guzheng's soundboard is typically paulownia wood. Density varies by origin and growth age, which directly affects resonance and tonal richness. Entry-level instruments often use pieced boards of ordinary paulownia, while professional performance guzhengs are hand-carved from a single high-grade plank for a clearer, longer-sustaining tone.

2. 琴弦材质影响手感与耐用度2. String Material Affects Feel & Durability

标准21弦古筝使用尼龙缠弦，价格实惠、手感柔和，适合初学者。若追求更明亮的音色，可考虑升级为钢丝尼龙弦，但需要更强的手指力度。无论哪种琴弦，都建议每6-12个月更换一次以保持音准。 Standard 21-string guzhengs use nylon-wound strings — affordable and gentle on fingers, ideal for beginners. For a brighter tone, steel-nylon strings are an option, though they require more finger strength. Whichever type you choose, replace strings every 6–12 months to keep pitch accurate.

3. 是否需要一步到位买专业琴？3. Should You Buy a Professional Instrument Right Away?

对于刚开始学习的孩子，我们通常建议从仲尼·志士或君子系列入门款开始，确认孩子确实有兴趣与毅力后，再于3-6级阶段升级到大贤或至圣系列的演奏级古筝。这样既能控制初期投入，也能避免因乐器限制而影响学习体验。 For a child just starting out, we usually recommend beginning with an entry-level model from the Zhongni Aspirant or Gentleman series, then upgrading to a Great Sage or Supreme Sage series performance instrument once genuine interest and commitment are confirmed — typically around Grade 3–6. This controls upfront cost while avoiding an instrument that holds back progress.

4. 品牌与售后同样重要4. Brand & After-Sales Support Matter Too

古筝需要定期调音、维修与保养，购买前请确认卖家是否提供售后支持。逸梅草堂所有古筝均附带调音工具，并可安排到馆检修与保养咨询。 A guzheng needs periodic tuning, repair, and maintenance, so confirm your seller offers after-sales support before buying. Every guzheng from YMCT comes with a tuning tool, and we offer in-studio servicing and maintenance advice.
