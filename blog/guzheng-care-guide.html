新加坡潮湿气候下的古筝保养指南 Guzheng Care in Singapore's Humid Climate

新加坡常年高温高湿，对以桐木为主要材质的古筝而言是不小的考验。湿度变化会导致面板膨胀或收缩，进而影响音准与音色。以下是几个简单却有效的保养习惯。 Singapore's year-round heat and humidity are tough on a paulownia-wood instrument. Humidity swings cause the soundboard to expand or contract, throwing off pitch and tone. Here are a few simple but effective habits to protect yours.

1. 避免阳光直射与空调风口1. Keep It Away From Direct Sun and Aircon Vents

阳光直射会使木料迅速失水开裂，而空调出风口的冷热交替同样会加速木材老化。建议将古筝放置在阴凉、通风良好但避免正对空调出风口的位置。 Direct sunlight dries out and cracks the wood quickly, while the constant temperature swings near an aircon vent accelerate wear. Place your guzheng in a shaded, well-ventilated spot that isn't directly in the airflow of an air conditioner.

2. 控制室内湿度在45%-65%之间2. Keep Indoor Humidity Between 45%–65%

湿度过高容易使木料膨胀变形，过低则可能导致开裂。若居住环境湿度波动较大，可考虑使用除湿机或在琴房内放置湿度计监测。 Excess humidity swells and warps the wood; too little causes cracking. If your home's humidity fluctuates a lot, consider a dehumidifier or keep a hygrometer in the practice room to monitor conditions.

3. 定期检查与更换琴弦3. Check and Replace Strings Regularly

潮湿环境容易使琴弦氧化生锈，影响音色亮度。建议每6-12个月更换一套琴弦，弹奏后可用软布轻拭琴弦表面的汗渍与灰尘。联系我们可为您的古筝安排更换服务。 Humidity accelerates string oxidation, dulling tone brightness. Replace your strings every 6–12 months, and wipe them down with a soft cloth after playing to remove sweat and dust. Contact us to arrange a restring for your instrument.

4. 使用防潮琴包妥善运输与存放4. Use a Moisture-Resistant Bag for Transport & Storage

若需要携带古筝出门上课或演出，务必使用加厚防潮琴包，避免碰撞与潮气渗入。存放时应立放而非平放，减少面板长期受压变形的风险。 If you need to transport your guzheng to a class or performance, always use a thickly padded, moisture-resistant bag to prevent knocks and moisture ingress. Store it upright rather than flat to reduce the risk of long-term pressure warping the soundboard.
